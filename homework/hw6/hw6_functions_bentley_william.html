Paragraph

  • I found that the results show that the Hawiian method of syllabification works correctly for the word “banana”, but not for either “splash” or “strengths”. Banana follows a consonant-vowel pattern that makes it easier to separate into syllables. “Splash” and “strengths” contain conanants that are bined together according to their number of syllables, so the Hawaiian syllabification method in unable to account for them. Multiple consonants occur at the beginning or end of a syllable in “splash” and “strengths”, which doesn’t correspond to English pronounciation. Because Hawaiian syllables consist or either a single vowel or a consonant follwed by a vowel, this is not a problem for Hawaiian using these rules. This shows that English permits mre complex syllabic structures that Hawaiian does.
